Open a shop.

Mini-Shopify lets merchants set up an online store in minutes and lets customers discover shops by name or category.

For merchants

Create a shop, tag it with categories, and list products with a description, picture and stock count.

For customers

Search shops by name or category, browse their catalogue, and fill a cart with whatever is in stock.

Simulated checkout

Purchases are simulated, but they relate with inventory: you can never buy more than a shop has.
